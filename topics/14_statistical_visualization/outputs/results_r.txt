
============================================================
WORKED EXAMPLE: choosing the right chart
============================================================
One numeric variable        -> histogram / density / boxplot
Numeric by category         -> boxplot / violin
Two numeric variables       -> scatter plot (+ regression line)
Numeric over time           -> line graph
Counts of a category        -> bar chart

INTERPRETATION
============================================================
- The histogram shows an approximately bell-shaped score distribution.
- Boxplot/violin show method C shifts the whole distribution upward.
- The scatter plot shows a positive linear relationship between study hours and score.
- Truncating a bar chart's axis exaggerates differences: always start bars at zero.
