INTERPRETATION
============================================================
- Population skewness 2.23; sample-mean skewness falls from 1.56 (n=2) to 0.27 (n=100).
- Mean of sample means equals mu; their SD matches sigma/sqrt(n); quadrupling n halves the SE.
