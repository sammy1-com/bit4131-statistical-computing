5. Conclusions

  1. Teaching method matters: mean scores differ significantly across methods (p = 5.3e-07).
  2. Study hours is the strongest single predictor (r = 0.71); each extra hour adds about 1.69 marks, controlling for the other factors.
  3. The model explains 64% of score variation; the remainder is individual variation not measured here.
  4. The data are synthetic: conclusions illustrate the method, not real students.
